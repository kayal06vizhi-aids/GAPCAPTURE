Data Platform Engineer


We are seeking a Data Platform Engineer to manage and optimize our data infrastructure, ensuring data availability, reliability, and performance for analytics and ML workloads.

Responsibilities:

Design and maintain scalable data pipelines and ETL processes
Work with cloud-based data platforms (AWS, GCP, Azure)
Ensure data quality, consistency, and governance
Collaborate with data scientists and analysts to support ML and BI initiatives
Monitor and troubleshoot data platform issues

Requirements:

Strong knowledge of SQL, Python, and data warehousing
Experience with ETL tools and pipeline orchestration (Airflow, Prefect)
Familiarity with big data technologies (Spark, Hadoop)
Knowledge of cloud storage, data lakes, and data pipelines

Nice to Have:

Experience with ML pipelines and MLOps tools
Knowledge of Docker and containerization
Understanding of analytics and visualization tools (Tableau, Power BI)

Key Skills:

SQL, Python, ETL, Data Warehousing, Airflow, Prefect, Spark, Hadoop, AWS, GCP, Azure, Data Lakes, Docker, MLOps, Tableau, Power BI
